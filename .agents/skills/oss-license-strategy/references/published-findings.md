# Published findings

Published claims with their sources, the self-set baselines used where no published figure exists, and the claims this skill refuses to make. Read it before quoting any figure, threshold or precedent: a maintainer about to change a license will ask "says who?", and the honest answer differs line by line.

## Claims deliberately not made

- What a court would decide in any specific dispute, including whether a particular architecture triggers AGPL's network clause.
- Any percentage of enterprises that ban AGPL. Name the published policies instead (Google's), rather than quoting a share of the market.
- Jurisdiction-specific rules - moral rights, employee-invention statutes, enforceability of public-domain dedications. The skill is deliberately jurisdiction-neutral and routes these to counsel.
- Whether a specific employment contract assigns a contributor's work to their employer.
- Any adoption, download or star effect attributed to a license choice.
